About me

Experienced pre-sales leader driving scalable business growth by leading high-performing technical solution engineering teams. I build teams that sell with their sales partners, not behind them, and I lead with data, trust and clear priorities.

I have spent 9+ years at Salesforce, two in Ireland and more than 7 in North America. I started as an SMB Solution Engineer covering the DACH market, moved into ECS (the run-rate of Enterprise), and have led solutions teams across TMT, CBS and Strategic AI Specialists before taking on Data Foundations for Retail enterprise.

Outside of work, I rebalance through dance and travel. Sustainability is a true passion of mine, which is why I give my time to Earthforce as its Global Executive Vice President.

Leadership philosophy

Be the change you want to see.

Leading self

Excellence, trust and integrity. I hold myself to the standard I ask of the team.

Leading others

Ownership, empowerment and respect. People grow fastest when they own real problems.

Leading the business

Execution matters most. Clear priorities, a data-driven focus and tight alignment with sales.

A flourishing, high-performing organization

Where results and culture reinforce each other.

Three cornerstones of a solutions leader

Run the business

Assess skill gaps, uplevel the team, and bring proactive risk calls to the forecast with the sales leaders.

Impact the big deals

Identify the deals that matter, validate the problem, secure the technical win and put the right specialists in the room.

Provide thought leadership

Give the team and customers a clear point of view, from platform advantage to AI and data readiness.
